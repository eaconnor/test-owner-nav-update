# Vision — (Test) Console Navigation

## Direction

People managing devices and tenants should always know where they are and get to the next task in one click. Navigation exists to serve that — not to showcase everything the product can do.

## What This Is Not

Not a place to relitigate the whole information architecture every time a team ships one feature. Features network into this file; they don't get their own vision.md.
